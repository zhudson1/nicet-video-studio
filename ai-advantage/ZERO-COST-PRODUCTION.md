# Zero-cost production policy

No subscriptions, paid generation credits, paid stock, paid publishing tools or paid rendering. Prefer existing GitHub Actions (subject to quotas), FFmpeg, Pillow, original phone recordings, original screen captures, locally generated graphics, and individually verified free commercial-use stock licenses. No Everygen.

## Production priority
1. Original demonstration footage and accurate script.
2. Strong first 2 seconds; compelling question and visual contrast.
3. Clean mobile-safe captions, precise sound design and purposeful cuts.
4. Commercial-use licensed background footage only, with source ledger.
5. Human quality approval before publishing.

## Implementation sequence
- Run existing manually triggered AI Advantage renderer for episode 01 and inspect output.
- Upgrade slideshow renderer to footage-based scene timeline with moving crops, layered typography, captions and transitions.
- Replace eSpeak draft voice with an acceptable free local voice or recorded narration after A/B listening test.
- Create reusable per-episode manifest with original footage paths, rights records and scripts.
- Export final 1080x1920 MP4 for TikTok and Reels; preserve 720x1280 fallback for runner limits.
- Keep publishing manual until official integrations are configured and tested.

## Budget guardrail
Never silently enable paid APIs or cloud services. Free tiers may have quotas. Avoid claiming generated photorealism when using stock footage. No final video is approved until visual, audio, factual and licensing checks pass.
