# Episode 01 — How a Fire Alarm Control Module Works

**Format:** 9:16 portrait, target 35–45 seconds, English

## Voiceover (natural, confident, practical)
"Ever wonder how a fire alarm panel tells another piece of equipment what to do?

Meet the control module.

When the panel's programmed conditions are met, it sends a command to the compatible addressable module.

Depending on the specific module, the output might switch relay contacts—or control a supervised output circuit.

That output can interface with other equipment, but it doesn't mean the module supplies all the power that equipment needs.

And here's the important part: wiring, supervision, and allowed applications depend on the exact model and approved design.

That's control modules in under a minute."
