# Episode 01 — NOTIFIER FCM-1 vs FRM-1: control output or relay?
Status: production script, requires model-accurate visual review before release.
Target: 9:16, ~40–50 seconds. No autopublishing.

## Narration
"These two NOTIFIER modules may look similar, but they do different jobs.
The FCM-1 is a supervised control module. A compatible fire alarm panel commands it over the signaling line circuit. It can control a listed notification or control circuit, using an appropriate external power source.
The FRM-1 is different. It's an addressable relay module, providing two Form-C dry-contact sets that operate together. Those contacts can interface with compatible equipment controls.
Here's the key: a dry contact isn't a power supply, and a supervised control output isn't the same as a dry-contact relay.
Always check the exact module's instructions, ratings, and approved drawings before wiring or programming."
