# NICET VIDEO STUDIO — Cinematic Production Template v1

**Master format:** 1080 × 1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 40–50 seconds. Export H.264 MP4 with AAC audio. Keep all essential titles and captions within center 80% of frame; avoid the top/bottom interface overlays.

## Visual language
- **Background:** midnight navy #07131F, secondary steel #142A3A.
- **Accent:** signal cyan #29D3D0 for communication; alert amber #F7B548 for activated output; red #E24C52 ONLY for genuine alarm/fault context.
- **Typography:** modern sans-serif, bold condensed-style titles, high-contrast captions. Do not use stylized pseudo-brand text.
- **Camera:** macro hardware shot → smooth 3D orbit → slow push-in → clean technical diagram → hero close.
- **Lighting:** studio softbox with edge/rim lighting; realistic metal/plastic textures; no glowing wires presented as literal electrical current.
- **Audio:** human-quality voiceover, restrained ambient soundtrack below speech, light interface cues. No eSpeak voice in final releases.
- **Captions:** 2 lines maximum, roughly 5–7 words per line; highlight only key terms.
- **Technical overlay:** label all non-literal circuit graphics 'CONCEPTUAL — NOT FIELD WIRING'.

## Six-scene repeatable timeline
| Time | Scene | Motion | On-screen treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Hook | Fast macro dolly | 4–7 word curiosity hook |
| 5–12 s | Device introduction | Controlled orbit | Verified device name and function |
| 12–20 s | Input or communication | Conceptual signal animation | One precise claim |
| 20–30 s | Output/action | Animated sectional cutaway or separate schematic | Distinguish physical and symbolic |
| 30–40 s | Real-world application | Match cut to equipment | One documented example |
| 40–48 s | Recap and CTA | Slow hero push | 'CHECK MODEL • RATINGS • DRAWINGS' |

## Generic device asset rules
1. Use realistic, manufacturer-neutral fire alarm devices with no logos, model numbers, or copied trade dress.
2. Do not fabricate specific terminal designations, ratings, listings, or installation diagrams.
3. Keep electrical functions technically correct; a supervised output and dry-contact relay are different functions.
4. Label all conceptual signal-flow or circuit visuals 'CONCEPTUAL — NOT FIELD WIRING'.
5. Verify general principles against authoritative manufacturer instructions and applicable standards; don't attribute generic devices to a brand.
