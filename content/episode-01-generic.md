# Episode 01 — How a Fire Alarm Control Module Works
**Device policy:** generic unbranded hardware; no manufacturer-specific labels or wiring.
**Length:** 40–45 seconds; 9:16 vertical; six scenes.

## Narration
"How does a fire alarm panel tell other equipment what to do?
An addressable control module receives commands from a compatible fire alarm control panel over its signaling line circuit.
Depending on its listed design, a control module may operate a supervised output circuit using an appropriate external power source.
A relay module is different: its dry contacts can switch an external control circuit, but those contacts don't provide equipment power by themselves.
The exact wiring, supervision, and permitted uses depend on the module and approved design.
That's the difference between sending a command and controlling an output."
